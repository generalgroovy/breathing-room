Benefits & guidance +

Comfort comes first

Sit or lie comfortably. Keep breaths quiet and an ordinary, comfortable size. There is no lung-volume target: do not force your lungs full or empty. The orb guides timing, not breath size. Counts are optional; let any cue pass if needed. NHS guidance and CUH breathing guidance.

Choose Own rhythm for a quiet timer without breathing instructions. Holds are optional, not a goal or a promise of extra benefit. Remove them whenever uncomfortable.

Stop if dizzy, tingly, breathless, anxious or uncomfortable. Return to your usual breathing. Do not practise while driving or in water. Seek urgent medical help for chest pain, severe breathlessness or fainting.

Choose what suits you

This is general wellness practice for adults, not a children’s protocol. If you have a heart or lung condition, are pregnant, have fainting episodes or epilepsy, or breathing focus worsens anxiety, ask a clinician about paced breathing and holds before using them. NCCIH guidance.

Gentle practice may help relaxation and perceived stress; research quality varies. These sources do not establish one best ratio. This app is not clinically validated and does not monitor health, diagnose or treat conditions. Read the research review and breathing-speed trial.
