Benefits & guidance +

A small space for relaxation

Gentle, regular breathing may support relaxation and reduce how stressed you feel. Research is promising, but study quality varies. No single rhythm is established as best, and slower breathing is not automatically more helpful. These presets offer comfortable starting points, not special health formulas. See the research review and a trial comparing breathing speeds.

Comfort comes first

Sit or lie somewhere comfortable. Keep your breaths an ordinary, comfortable size; do not force a deep breath or try to fill your lungs. Start briefly and choose a pace that feels easy. Pauses are optional: skip them if uncomfortable.

Stop and return to natural breathing if you feel dizzy, anxious or uncomfortable. Do not practise while driving or in water. If you have a breathing or heart condition, or are unsure whether this practice suits you, ask a clinician for guidance.

This app supports personal practice and does not diagnose or treat a condition. More guidance: NHS breathing exercises and NCCIH relaxation techniques.
